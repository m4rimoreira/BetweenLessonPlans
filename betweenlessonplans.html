Summary:

Lucy Chen is the kind of teacher who brings cookies to staff meetings and believes every student deserves a second chance. Tim Bradford runs the gym like a military base and thinks she's way too soft. They don't get along. At all. But between school fundraisers and meetings something starts to change.

Chapter 1 · Day one

The first hello ✨

Lucy hangs twinkle lights. Tim calls them a fire hazard. The war begins.

🎨
Chapter 2 · The fundraiser

Paint, glitter & chaos

Forced to organize the school fundraiser together. Blue paint happens. So does glitter. Neither survives clean.

👀
Chapter 3 · After the bell

The touch that lingered

Amid cleanup, their hands brush. Neither acknowledges it. Both feel it.

📝
Chapter 4 · The rumor mill

"Chenford" is born

The students start shipping them. A poster. A drawing. Absolute chaos in the hallway.

🚪
Chapter 5 · The principal's office

A truce is called

Andersen has a file. A whole file. Tim and Lucy shake hands. They both know it won't last ten minutes.

☕
Chapter 7 · An early morning

Coffee, made just right

Tim learns how she likes her coffee. Lucy pretends it doesn't mean anything. It does.

🍸
Chapter 8 · Las Torres

A story about the military

Tim tells her more than he's told anyone in years. A stranger named Chris shows up. Tim notices.

💡
Chapter 9 · The assembly

The almost-kiss

A hand on top of hers. A thumb tracing her skin. The janitor interrupts everything.

💭
Chapter 10 · The morning after

"Nothing happened"

He shuts it down. She shuts down. Thirteen days of silence begin.

💥
Chapter 12 · Outside Las Torres

The kiss

Weeks of tension snap. They kiss in the middle of the sidewalk, angry and desperate. Everything changes.

🔒
Chapter 13 & 14 · The storage room

Locked in together

A jammed door. A fight. Another kiss. Then a real conversation — and a real question. She says yes.

💌
Chapter 15 · The day after

Saturday at 8

Gossip everywhere. A coffee made just for her. And a date that neither of them can wait for.
